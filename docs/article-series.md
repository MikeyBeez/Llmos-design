# Article Series

Planned Medium articles, one per idea. Each one stands alone and links back here.

**Published**

- "Agents Should Be Users." Agents as OS users under their human owner. (Piece 1)

**Planned**

1. **"A Prompt Is Not a Permission."** Why rules in the prompt fail and rules in a checker hold. The Unix system call as the model. (Piece 3)
2. **"Your Agent Shouldn't Get Every Tool."** Tools as files with owners. Why web search shouldn't come free. (Piece 2)
3. **"Fill Out the Form."** Forms instead of free commands. Keeping the model outside the trust boundary. (Piece 5)
4. **"Two Agents, One File."** Why agents need locks more than programs do: they think slowly. (Piece 4)
5. **"Who Can Change the Rules?"** Elevation as a permission, and the open question of who grants it. (Piece 6)
6. **"Safe Out of the Box."** Defaults, and who should set them, including the case for the law. (Piece 7)
7. **"Build the Org Chart Into the Agent."** Roles set at build time. (Piece 8)
8. **"Don't Load Every Tool."** Tool trees and side context windows. (Piece 9)
9. **"Chat That Can't Touch Anything."** A fast lane that's safe because it has no hands. (Piece 10)
10. **"A Wall Is Not an Owner."** Sandbox vs. OS. (sandbox-vs-os page)
11. **"Ten Ways Agents Go Wrong."** The attacks page as an article. (attacks page)
12. **"Earning Access."** Track record over time as the way to grant agents and people more power, and why it guards against big-lab capture of licensing. (Open question 3)

**Suggested order:** 1, 3, 10, 11 first. They make the case for the whole idea. The rest fill in pieces.
